# Becoming a Blue-Buttoner

(This is the personal story behind [The Blue Button](/pages/blue_button).)

It's funny that [language models choose the blue button over the red when making the initial decision, but then choose red after reasoning it through](https://softmax.com/blog/red-button-blue-button).

This is the opposite of my choice.

My initial reasoning was simple -- i apply [superrationality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superrationality). i think for all minds like me. We press red -> minds like me survive.

Then a friend pointed out that there are mistakes. there are children. there are minds not like mine. and pressing blue is a wager: minds + minds like mine on the line; we lose we cede the world to the red-pressers. we win we live in a blue-button world. (This was sometime in 2023 — [the discourse started with a Reddit poll in April 2023](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/red-button-or-blue-button-question), and went viral again on Twitter in April 2026.)

I nodded along at the time but i eventually read the 3bp and watched the [supercoordination cluster video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X6MYsKeTjKk).

and i think by now i am a solid blue-buttoner.
