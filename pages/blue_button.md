# The Blue Button

When the Trisolarians invade; when you know humanity is done for; when you're the [Swordholder](https://muratbuffalo.blogspot.com/2020/01/deaths-end-by-liu-cixin-and-swordholder.html) with the button that destroys their homeworld and yours.

Do you press it?

When [everybody gets offered a red and blue button](https://www.goodthoughts.blog/p/buttons-blenders-and-coordination). Red guarantees your own survival. Blue lets you wager your life for a chance at everybody's, children and idiots and strangers insane...

Do you press it?

At the end of the 3 Body Problem series, [Cheng Xin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death%27s_End) gives up her pocket universe in hope to remake the cosmos a happier, friendly, warmer place.

Would you let yours go?

Three choices.

Dominant game-theoretic strategy, or some idiotic wager on a hope?

Live in a cold dark forest where defection crowds out love? Die for a concept of a vision of a hope of being somewhere else?

Read more about my choice [here](/pages/becoming_a_bluebuttoner).
